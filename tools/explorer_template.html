What is modelled

  • 1 m wide strip in the x–z plane, lumped masses every 25 mm
  • Axial springs (EA), panel bending (EI), fold hinges with rest angle (fold memory)
  • Spring-damper attachments at root and tip; prescribed tip motion
  • Optional gravity and quadratic air drag

What is not

  • The mechanism, and any two-way coupling with it
  • Contact between stack layers, friction, the stowage box
  • Creep in the folds (hysteresis is an optional law); air added mass
  • Anything out of plane: twist, edge effects, individual cell strips

How far to trust it

Numerics are verified against analytic cases: pendulum period to 0.08%, hinge frequency to 0.34%, energy balance closed to about 10⁻⁶, and peak loads converged in mesh and time step. The physics inputs are not: every stiffness, damping ratio and allowable is a placeholder. Read trends and rankings, not magnitudes.
